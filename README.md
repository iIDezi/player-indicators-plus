# Player Indicators Plus

Player Indicators Plus gives you more control over how nearby players are
highlighted. Names and colors can change when another player enters or leaves a
Wilderness or PvP area, even if you stay in a safe area.

## Features

- Separate colors for your player, party members, friends, friends chat, team members, clan
  members, and everyone else.
- Different friend and player colors inside combat zones.
- Optional names above players, colored tiles, minimap labels, and right-click
  menu colors.
- Friends chat and clan rank icons.
- RuneLite color pickers for every category.

## Party members

Under **Highlight options**, **Highlight party members** is enabled by default.
Choose **Party member** to change its color (default `#EA7B5B`). This uses your
current RuneLite party, including Party Hub parties, and updates as members
join, leave, or log out.

Party color applies to enabled names, tiles, minimap labels, and player menu
colors. It takes priority over friend, clan, team, and combat-zone colors.
Your own character still follows the own-player settings. Disabling party
highlighting restores the normal category colors.

The plugin only changes how players are displayed. It does not change clicks,
combat options, or game input.
